\subsection{Data and Code Availability}

\begin{itemize}
    \item All data reported in this paper have been deposited at Zenodo and are publicly available as of the date of publication under a CC-BY 4.0 licence with no access restrictions: \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21840194}. The deposit comprises the gold-standard dataset of 1,000 titles, abstracts, and author-written highlights; all generated summaries together with per-paper and per-model metric scores across the lexical, semantic, and factual dimensions; the raw ratings of all eight expert evaluators; the \ac{LLM}-as-a-judge panel scores; the data leakage analyses; and the aggregated ranking, concordance, and agreement tables reported in the figures and tables.
    \item All original code has been deposited at Zenodo alongside the data and is publicly available as of the date of publication under an MIT licence: \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21840194}. Development versions are maintained at \url{https://github.com/Delta4AI/LLMTextSummarizationBenchmark}.
    \item Any additional information required to reanalyze the data reported in this paper is available from the lead contact upon request.
\end{itemize}
